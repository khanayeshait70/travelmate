TravelMate Pakistan is a destination discovery and recommendation project designed to make exploring Pakistan more personal and accessible.

01

About TravelMate

Pakistan is rich in natural beauty, culture, history and unforgettable experiences. TravelMate brings destination information and personalized recommendations together in one place. Visitors can explore places, narrow choices by budget and city, select interests, and share feedback.

02

How it works

The website sends your selected filters to a FastAPI backend. The backend loads the project dataset and calls the existing recommendation function. Results are returned to the website with destination details and matching images. Feedback is stored in the existing SQLite database.
